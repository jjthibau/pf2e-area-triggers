# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial public release of native area and turn prompts.
- Reviewed PF2e 8.6.0 support for 388 aura families, 140 canonical spells and 133 other qualifying ability families.
- Entry, movement, turn-boundary, source-turn, Sustain, activation and scheduled prompts where defined by the source.
- GM live/pause control for traps and haunts; no automatic exploration prompts.
- Native saves and damage, manually linked Regions, source snapshots, NPC adjustments and vertical following.
- Conditions, immunities, exemptions, action expenditure and special riders remain table managed.
